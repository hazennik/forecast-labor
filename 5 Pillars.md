✅ PILLAR 2 — The Model Architecture (Elite Accuracy)

High-end macro desks use a combination of econometric + ML + revision modeling.

To reach top 1%, you need all three working together.

✅ 1. The “Structural Engine” — Dynamic Factor Model (DFM)

Purpose: fuse dozens of signals into one labor “state.”
Strengths: stability, low noise, best for turning point detection.

✅ 2. The “High-Frequency Engine” — MIDAS Regression

Purpose: convert weekly/daily data → monthly prediction.
Strengths: reacts instantly to new claims & withholdings.

✅ 3. The “Nonlinear Engine” — XGBoost / LightGBM Quantile Model

Purpose: add non-linear corrections the government misses:
	•	weather distortions
	•	strike impacts
	•	extreme tail events
	•	shifting seasonal patterns

✅ 4. The “Revision Engine” (critical for elite performance)

Purpose: predict how much the FIRST print will revise.
Strengths: gives you more accurate true reading of the labor market.

You now become better than the BLS.

✅ 5. Calibration Layer (for SN41)

Purpose: transform raw predictions → perfectly calibrated probabilities.
Tools:
	•	Isotonic regression
	•	Conformal prediction intervals
	•	Platt scaling for classification-style bins

This makes you SN41 optimized.
